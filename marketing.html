The way it works.

A small list of flats, kept by the people who live in them. No algorithm. No instant book. A conversation, then a key.

01

You find a stay.

Browse the list. Each flat is described the way a host would describe it — what the kitchen smells like in the morning, whether the desk gets the afternoon light.

02

You write to the host.

No instant booking. You write a short note about who you are and what you're working on. The host reads it. They write back, usually within a day.

03

You stay, then you know.

The flat is yours. The host is nearby if you need them, invisible if you don't. By the end of the second week, you'll know the bakery's name.
