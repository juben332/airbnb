— The flat, as Marta would describe it

The fishmonger downstairs opens at six. By seven the street smells like the sea, by eight like espresso, by ten like bread. You stop noticing by the end of the first week, and then you start noticing again, the way you notice things you're about to miss.

The flat is on the second floor — there is no lift, and the stairs creak on the third and seventh step. The ceiling is high, the windows are tall, and the kitchen is smaller than it looks in photographs, which is to say exactly the right size.

I left a list of places on the table. Not the ones in the guide. The ones I actually go to. The bakery on Rua de São Bento opens at 7:30 and sells out of the almond croissants by 8:15. You have been warned.
